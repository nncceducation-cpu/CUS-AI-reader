Add an independently labeled case

Upload the study in “Read a study,” then record what you verified. Unknown findings contribute no positive or negative training label. Category tags organize your cases; training uses the observable findings below.

Keep every serial scan from one infant in the same partition. Use unseen infants for the holdout and an independent center for external testing. A normal category tag does not automatically mark every finding absent.

Prefill preserves unknown laterality and does not invent measurements, negative findings in other domains, or serial evidence. Review the findings before saving.

My private case library

A backup contains resized training frames, labels and any learned weights. It is private research data; keep it outside public repositories.

Train a new research model

This trains actual convolutional image-model weights from your labeled studies. Every frame enters the model; frame features are pooled into a study representation. Labels remain separate for each observable finding and hemisphere.

Start with at least four independent training infants. Each learned finding needs two positive and two negative training infants. The holdout is excluded from fitting; external test cases are excluded from both fitting and promotion.

Browser learning uses 64 × 64 grayscale frames and a small CNN trained from scratch. Resolution can obscure small lesions, and small datasets can overfit. This is a development tool, not established diagnostic accuracy.

Model versions and measured performance

A version can become your active research model only after every learned finding reaches 80% sensitivity and specificity on the development holdout, with at least four holdout infants and two positive and two negative studies per finding. A replacement also needs at least two percentage points improvement over the active model on the same holdout. These development gates do not establish clinical validity.
