Research use
Unvalidated AI feasibility suggestion. Research and workflow testing only.

This reader learns from your labeled cases. No validated diagnostic model is preinstalled. Learned findings are research estimates, and cannot establish a normal study or replace expert review.

Cases and learned models stay in this browser on this device. Export a private backup before clearing browser storage. Do not upload identifiable media.

Review a cranial ultrasound study

Images, image sets, MP4/MOV clips, and NIfTI sequences including 2D-plus-time cines. Every frame is decoded; long studies exceeding the browser budget are rejected rather than sampled.

Preprocessing options

Frames are letterboxed, never stretched, so source aspect ratio is preserved. Sector cropping is estimated once per source and is skipped when it would discard most of the frame. Band blanking is a de-identification aid, not a guarantee: it cannot read text or recognise a logo.

Maximum 256 frames per study and 256 MB per source. DICOM, AVI, MKV and TIFF are supported by the Python reader.

Verified GMH-IVH classification

Enter expert-verified findings and calibrated AHW. These paper rules do not change when the model learns. AI scores never fill this form.

This form covers GMH-IVH only. PVHI, WMI, CBH, PHVD, and complete normal-study assessment require the full expert workflow in the Python reader and appropriate serial evidence.

Add an independently labeled case

Upload the study in “Read a study,” then record what you verified. Unknown findings contribute no positive or negative training label. Category tags organize your cases; training uses the observable findings below.

Keep every serial scan from one infant in the same partition. Use unseen infants for the holdout and an independent center for external testing. A normal category tag does not automatically mark every finding absent.

Prefill preserves unknown laterality and does not invent measurements, negative findings in other domains, or serial evidence. Choosing “not recorded” fills only the any-side findings and leaves both hemispheres unknown. Review the findings before saving.

My private case library

A backup contains resized training frames, labels and any learned weights. It is private research data; keep it outside public repositories.

Train a new research model

This trains actual convolutional image-model weights from your labeled studies. Every frame enters inference; frame features are pooled into a study representation by learned attention. Labels remain separate for each observable finding and hemisphere.

Start with at least four independent training infants. Each learned finding needs two positive and two negative training infants. The holdout is excluded from fitting and from threshold and epoch selection; external test cases are excluded from both fitting and promotion.

The frame cap applies to training steps only; inference always uses every decoded frame. Mirroring is offered for coronal views alone: in a parasagittal view the horizontal axis is anterior-posterior, so a mirror would reverse anatomy without exchanging hemispheres.

Browser learning uses grayscale frames at the selected edge and a small CNN trained from scratch. Resolution can obscure small lesions, and small datasets can overfit. This is a development tool, not established diagnostic accuracy.

Model versions and measured performance

A version becomes development-qualified only when every learned finding holds a 95% Wilson lower bound at or above 80% sensitivity and specificity on the development holdout, and a replacement also needs an infant-clustered bootstrap lower bound above zero against the active model. A version whose point estimates clear 80% on a holdout too small to bound is marked exploratory and may be used for workflow testing only. Neither tier establishes clinical validity.

Self-tests

These run in this browser, on this device. The statistical vectors were generated from reference implementations (statsmodels for Wilson intervals, scikit-learn for ROC AUC and Brier score) and are embedded as fixed expectations. The learning smoke test fits a few epochs on synthetic noise to confirm the model graph executes here.
